🎯 Project Mission & Policy Mandate

Aligning National Statistical Datasets with Rwanda's NST2 & Vision 2050 Climate Directives

POLICY CONTEXT

MINAGRI & RAB SSIT Subsidies

Under the National Strategy for Transformation (NST2), the Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources (MINAGRI) and the Rwanda Agriculture & Animal Resources Board (RAB) subsidize Small-Scale Irrigation Technology (SSIT) solar pumps by up to 50%. AgriShield provides the data-driven spatial targeting engine to maximize return on public investment.

THE PROBLEM

Climate Drought Risk & Rainfed Dependence

Over 845,000 hectares of smallholder farmland rely strictly on unpredictable seasonal rains. In dry corridor regions like Eastern Province (Bugesera, Kayonza) and high-poverty Southern sectors (Gisagara, Nyaruguru), unmitigated dry spells trigger yield failures and exacerbate rural poverty.

OUR SOLUTION

Multi-Criteria Decision Support

AgriShield replaces manual guesswork with a transparent multi-criteria optimization formula ($U_i = w_1 \cdot \text{Climate} + w_2 \cdot \text{Cropland} + w_3 \cdot \text{Poverty}$) with a strict $w_1 + w_2 + w_3 = 1.00$ live constraint rule, enabling policy makers to dynamically balance climate risk, smallholder area, and poverty.

📡 Data Sources & Grounding

  • NISR SAS (Seasonal Agricultural Survey): Ingests 2026/2025 release crop area, irrigation share, and yield benchmarks.
  • NISR EICV (Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey): Official household poverty microdata rates across all 30 districts and 422 sectors.
  • CHIRPS Satellite Rainfall: Daily/monthly precipitation anomaly indices.
  • ECMWF ERA5 Reanalysis: Land surface temperatures and dry spell durations (days).
  • Copernicus Topsoil Moisture: Volumetric water content ($m^3/m^3$) measuring root-zone moisture deficits.

⚡ Predictive Modeling & TreeSHAP

  • StratifiedKFold Cross-Validation: Solves single-class fold collapse by stratifying climate stress labels across all folds.
  • Dynamic Prior Thresholding: Optimizes F1 scores matching the empirical target prior ($\approx 0.40$).
  • TreeSHAP Explainability: Quantifies exact feature impacts (dry spell days, topsoil moisture, poverty rate) for every sector.
  • Zero Data Fallback: 100% reliable local NLP fallback engine for instant stats lookup.
