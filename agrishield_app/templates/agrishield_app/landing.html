Solving Rwanda's Smallholder Irrigation Gap

Seasonal Dry Spell Vulnerability

Over 845,000 hectares of smallholder farmland rely entirely on seasonal rainfall. Prolonged dry spells in Eastern and Southern Rwanda severely impair crop yields and threaten rural food security.

Targeted SSIT Solar Pump Subsidies

Under Rwanda's NST2 strategy, MINAGRI subsidizes small-scale solar irrigation by up to 50%. AgriShield replaces manual distribution with transparent, multi-criteria data-driven targeting.

Explainable Machine Learning

Combining XGBoost and LightGBM models trained on NISR microdata with TreeSHAP explainability, every priority score is fully traceable back to empirical climate, crop, and poverty factors.
