1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & POLICY MANDATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Platform Name: NISR AgriShield Rwanda (Rwanda Agricultural Climate Resilience & SSIT Solar Pump Prioritization Engine).
- Built For: National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) Hackathon 2026.
- Official Brand Palette: Deep Navy (#071328), NISR Official Gold (#F4B41A), Electric Cyan (#149BFC).
- Policy Target: Optimizes the spatial targeting of Small-Scale Irrigation Technology (SSIT) solar pump subsidies managed by MINAGRI (Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources) and RAB (Rwanda Agriculture & Animal Resources Board) under NST2 (National Strategy for Transformation 2) and Vision 2050.
- Agricultural Scope: Total Rwanda cropland area = 896,180 hectares across 30 districts and 422 sectors. Unirrigated rainfed cropland = 845,988 hectares (~94.4% rainfed exposure).

2. MACHINE LEARNING & MULTI-CRITERIA OPTIMIZATION ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Predictive Models: Multi-layer XGBoost & LightGBM trained using Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation (StratifiedKFold) on multi-modal raster and tabular data.
- Model Performance Metrics:
  * District Level: ROC-AUC = 0.8426 (F1 Score = 0.8462, Accuracy = 0.8667).
  * Sector Level: ROC-AUC = 0.9873 (F1 Score = 0.9739, Accuracy = 0.9787).
- Multi-Criteria Optimization Formula:
  Utility (Ui) = w1 * ClimateRisk + w2 * CroplandExposure + w3 * PovertyVulnerability
- STRICT WEIGHT CONSTRAINT RULE:
  w1 + w2 + w3 MUST EQUAL EXACTLY 1.00 (100%).
  If sum != 1.00, user submission is blocked in the UI with a red warning badge.

3. WEBSITE NAVIGATION & UI FEATURE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Customize Utility Weights: Click "⚙️ Customize Weights" button in the navigation header or sidebar. A modal pop-up appears allowing inputs for Climate Risk (w1), Cropland Exposure (w2), and Poverty (w3). Live validation enforces w1 + w2 + w3 = 1.00.
- 3D Spatial Prioritization Map: Navigate to "🌐 3D Spatial Prioritization Map". Extruded 3D polygon height represents computed SSIT Priority Score. Bright Crimson Red polygons indicate targeted high-priority subsidy zones under budget ceiling K; Emerald Green polygons indicate baseline rainfed land.
- Province Filter & Level Selector: Use the Province dropdown (Eastern, Western, Northern, Southern, Kigali City) to zoom to specific regions. Toggle between District Level (30 units) for macro policy and Sector Level (422 units) for micro field deployment.
- Budget & Target Simulation: Adjust target pump ceiling (K, e.g. 500 pumps at $1,200/unit = $600,000 total budget) to view protected cropland area (ha) and targeted poverty population share.
- Policy Audit Export: Go to "📝 Policy Simulation & Export" tab to download official CSV operational field deployment schedules for MINAGRI / RAB officers.
